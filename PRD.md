# PRD: Post 009 — Time Series Forecasting

**AI Engineering Lab Series | Era 1: Classic Machine Learning**

## Objective
Teach ARIMA/SARIMA forecasting using two datasets with distinct temporal patterns.

## Datasets
| Dataset | Domain | Records | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Datacenter Power Demand | General (Infrastructure) | 17,520 | Hourly |
| Silicon Aging (BTI Vth Shift) | Post-Silicon Validation | 1,095 | Daily |

## Concepts Covered
Stationarity, ADF test, differencing, ACF/PACF, ARIMA(p,d,q), SARIMA, seasonal decomposition, forecast horizons, confidence intervals.
