\section{Project Overview}

I forecast time series data using ARIMA and SARIMA on two datasets: datacenter power demand and silicon aging. I executed stationarity tests, ACF/PACF analysis, and seasonal decomposition to optimize forecasts. The results showcase effective multi-horizon predictions and accurate error metrics.

\textbf{Stationarity:} Statistical properties of a time series remain constant over time.

\textbf{ADF Test:} Tests for stationarity in time series data.

\textbf{Differencing:} Used to remove trends by calculating differences between consecutive observations.

\textbf{ACF/PACF:} Analyze autocorrelation for better model order selection.

\textbf{ARIMA:} Combines autoregressive and moving average components for forecasting.

\textbf{SARIMA:} Extends ARIMA by incorporating seasonality in time series data.
